## Intent

A 60–90 second launch/demo video for TAKEOVER, a Nimiq Pay Mini App and
last-minute marketplace for released capacity. Show-it-as-is: the live
app's own captured screens are the video's assets. Warm, human, cinematic
— a design-studio piece, not a template tour. Voiceover (Kokoro af_heart)
plus karaoke captions throughout, per REFERENCE-ANALYSIS.md principles.

## Assets

- assets/nimiq-pay-segment.mp4 — owner-supplied phone recording of the live
  app (dark mode, wallet connect, claims); the wallet-signing beat source.
- assets/nimiq-pay-reference-video.mp4 — stylistic reference (not in the cut).
- capture/screenshots/full-page.png — captured live feed plate for scroll/pan.
- capture/screenshots/scroll-000.png, scroll-086.png, scroll-100.png —
  captured viewport shots.
- apps/web/public/og-image.png — current thumbnail (repo, not video dir).
- apps/web/public/icon-512.png — app icon (repo, not video dir).

## Customizations

- Karaoke captions with terracotta active-phrase highlight (reference
  principle 3, TAKEOVER palette).
- Nimiq Pay phone segment composited screen-within-screen (reference
  principle 4 + phase brief beat 6, option (a)).
- Dark-mode beat from the warm dark theme (coal/cocoa/parchment).
- End card: brand mark, live URL, MIT license note (reference principle 8).

## Notes

- Consumer language only in VO: no "smart contract", "on-chain",
  "blockchain", "DeFi".
- Never fabricate a transaction, wallet, or success state. Every UI pixel
  is a real screenshot or the owner recording.
- Pronunciation: "TAKEOVER" one word (first-syllable stress), "U-S-D-T"
  letter by letter, "NIM-ick".
- Motion: emil-design-eng craft rules — under 300ms, ease-out entrances,
  transform + opacity only, no transition:all. App motion tokens:
  out-strong cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1), ui 200ms, press 120ms.
- Autonomous mode: no board reviews, no preview gate questions; heads-up
  summaries in the phase report instead.
